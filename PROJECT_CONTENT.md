# Portfolio project content

## 01 — Land Surface Temperature Analysis of Kerala (2004–2024)
Research project using MODIS, Google Earth Engine and ArcGIS. Spatial and temporal analysis of LST and NDVI with maps, graphs and technical reporting.

## 02 — Paddy Field Mapping
Satellite-based agricultural mapping using Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical imagery, with index-based and machine-learning approaches.

## 03 — Heat & Cold Wave Mapping
ERA5-Land based long-term extreme-temperature mapping for Odisha, including threshold-based event detection and cumulative spatial analysis.

## 04 — Multi-year LULC Classification
Landsat-based supervised classification and multi-year comparison for district-scale environmental analysis.

## 05 — ARR Spatial Assessment
Plot-level feasibility, LULC checks, suitability assessment and spatial documentation for afforestation/reforestation/revegetation projects.

## 06 — AWD Monitoring
Sentinel-1 SAR based agricultural water-management monitoring and spatial feature development.

## 07 — Coastal Habitat & Shoreline Change
Landsat-based shoreline erosion/accretion and coastal land-cover analysis.

## 08 — Blue Carbon & Wetland Projects
Spatial support for tidal wetland and seagrass restoration and blue-carbon feasibility work.

## 09 — Soil Carbon & Land Management
Environmental spatial analysis supporting soil-carbon and agricultural land-management projects.

## 10 — Renewable Energy ESIA GIS
LULC, DEM, slope, drainage and environmental mapping for solar and wind projects.

## 11 — Flood Risk & Terrain Analysis
Integration of terrain, rainfall, soil and land/environmental layers for flood-analysis workflows.

## 12 — Geospatial Data QA & Automation
Python/GIS workflows for data cleaning, plot checks, coordinate handling, KML workflows, LULC processing and repetitive spatial analysis.


## Cartography samples
### Flood Vulnerability & ESIA — Gujarat
Preliminary assessment and ESIA mapping for a 7.5 MW solar project at Baiwada, Banaskantha district, Gujarat. Map communicates project site, core/buffer zones and flood vulnerability.

### LULC & Environmental Screening — Maharashtra
Environmental and Social Screening / ESIA mapping for a 100.8 MW wind power project in Sangli district, Maharashtra. Map communicates WTG locations, project/core/buffer zones and LULC classes.

### Odisha Extreme Heat Risk — 2005–2025
Thematic climate-risk cartography showing cumulative extreme heat days above 40°C across Odisha.

## MSc Dissertation
**Land Surface Temperature Analysis of Kerala for Two Decades Using MODIS Data** — MSc Environmental Science dissertation submitted to Pondicherry University, April 2025. The study covers selected years 2004, 2009, 2014, 2019 and 2024, with latitudinal and altitudinal LST analysis and NDVI analysis. The portfolio links the full dissertation PDF from the Selected Work and Contact sections.
